National News Network

A Region desk, not a press office

Region N3 is the Northwest Indiana report of National News Network. The coverage map runs from the Valparaiso area through Griffith to South Bend. The voice is third-party: concise, complete, and unwilling to invent a brief to look busy.

What we cover

  • Crime

    Courts, police, and public-safety records across the Region. Named suspects only when charging documents or on-the-record officials support it.

  • Politics

    City halls, county councils, the Statehouse, and federal offices that touch Northwest Indiana. Process first, then the claim.

  • Business & Tech

    Hiring, plants, logistics, and the tech and industrial employers that move the Region’s economy. Jobs and investment are reported as facts, not slogans.

  • Community

    Schools, civic groups, neighborhoods, and the public notices that do not fit a crime or politics brief.
